The "Second Brain" methodology is a framework for personal knowledge management, popularized by Tiago Forte.

## Core Principles

### Capture

Collect ideas, insights, and information from everything you consume. Don't try to remember everything — write it down in a trusted system.

### Organize

Use a simple organizational system. The PARA method suggests four categories:
- **Projects** — active initiatives with a deadline
- **Areas** — ongoing responsibilities
- **Resources** — reference material for future use
- **Archive** — completed or inactive items

### Distill

Summarize information progressively. Each time you revisit a note, highlight the key points. This makes notes more useful over time.

### Express

Use your captured knowledge to create new work. The value of a second brain comes from applying what you've learned.

## Progressive Summarization

A key technique is to layer highlights on your notes over time. Each time you return to a note, bold the most important sentences, then highlight the key phrases within those sentences.

This works naturally in Markdown with `**bold**` and `==highlight==` syntax.
